# Content and link reports

CATODO contains software and a directory of user-initiated links; it does not
host media. We nevertheless review credible reports about curated source links.

Please open a GitHub issue titled `Content report` containing:

1. your name or organisation and authority to act;
2. the precise CATODO source entry and upstream URL;
3. the protected work, policy, or right concerned;
4. enough information to verify the claim;
5. a good-faith statement that the disputed use is not authorised; and
6. a contact address for follow-up.

The maintainers may temporarily disable the source while checking the report,
ask for clarification, notify the upstream project, or remove the directory
entry. Removing an entry from CATODO cannot remove material hosted by a third
party; reports about upstream material should also be sent to that host.
