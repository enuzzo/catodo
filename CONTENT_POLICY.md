# CATODO content policy

CATODO is an open-source media player. The project does not host, create, sell,
or control third-party playlists, video streams, programme data, or broadcaster
logos.

## External sources

- Curated entries are links to independently maintained providers.
- No external playlist is fetched until the user confirms the import.
- An entry in the source directory is not a guarantee that every linked stream
  is lawful, available, safe, or accessible in every territory.
- Sources that require DRM circumvention, stolen credentials, geo-restriction
  bypasses, or secret tokens are not accepted.
- CATODO may disable a curated source while a credible report is investigated.

Users are responsible for confirming that they may access a source and its
content in their jurisdiction.

## Logos and programme data

Third-party marks are displayed only to identify channels. Their inclusion does
not imply sponsorship or affiliation. CATODO does not redistribute a permanent
logo pack. Implementations should prefer remote URLs or a short-lived technical
thumbnail cache with source and timestamp metadata.

Programme data may carry rights distinct from the software and from the tool
used to collect it. Do not redistribute EPG data without reviewing its source
terms.

## Reporting a source

Open a GitHub issue titled `Content report` and include the exact source URL,
the right or policy concerned, evidence that you are authorised to report it,
and a reliable way to contact you. Do not include private personal information
or credentials in a public issue.

This policy is operational guidance, not legal advice.
