# Theatre artwork and vintage-film curation

Reviewed 2026-09-22 for the existing private, noncommercial installation.
Publication was explicitly authorized in the follow-up request. The catalog
remains **22 works / 29 editions**; the vintage selections below are research,
not additional playable films or completed collection filters.

## Artwork decisions

All 22 cards and the selected-film stage have a local image. Two are official
DVD covers; ALMA uses a separately licensed observatory photograph. The other
19 are selected, unaltered film stills from the exact licensed editions.
They are catalog illustrations, not newly invented or misidentified posters.
The selection was inspected visually; complete images are shown with contain
sizing. No crop, recoloring, compositing, AI retouching or title overlay is baked
into an image. Extraction/size reduction/JPEG encoding are technical conversions.
Original subtitle text and letterboxing remain where embedded in the frame.

Image credits link to their sources on each card and in the stage. Image kind,
source and license are also disclosed in the source/credits panel. Assets load
from CATODO; browsing does not silently contact media providers. No complete
film or soundtrack is bundled. Software licensing does not relicense artwork.

For NoDerivatives stills, the operational basis is an unaltered excerpt used to
identify and illustrate the catalog record, not a modified version of the film;
see the [Creative Commons excerpt FAQ](https://creativecommons.org/faq/#can-i-reuse-an-excerpt-of-a-larger-work-that-is-licensed-with-the-noderivs-restriction).
This is an interpretation for this use, not a worldwide legal assurance or a
permission to create trailers/remixes. Film attribution and the exact original
license remain attached. ESO's express whole-film restriction is respected by
using separately licensed images under its [image policy](https://www.eso.org/public/outreach/copyright/).
The 2012 Europe to the Stars DVD cover was selected; the similarly named 2018
planetarium poster would identify a different work and was rejected.

## Image register

Exact original file URLs, dimensions, extraction seconds and SHA-256 are stored

## Proposed seasons — not active collections

The user's “monster of the swamp” may refer to **Creature from the Black Lagoon**
(1954), or possibly **Swamp Thing** (1982); identification is not settled.
The editorial direction is vintage creatures, drive-in atmosphere, strange
science, and Elvis musicals, with attractive period artwork where cleared.

| Season | Editorial candidates | Current decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Midnight Monsters | Night of the Living Dead (1968); Carnival of Souls (1962) | Strong first research pair. US public-domain evidence exists, but territory, exact file, original score and image rights still need a complete edition review. Neither added in this release. |
| Drive-in After Dark | The Blob (1958); Creature from the Black Lagoon (1954); The Little Shop of Horrors (1960); House on Haunted Hill (1959) | The first two are reference titles with commercial rights-holder editions, not cleared sources. The latter two are research leads, not verified public-domain approvals. |
| Elvis on Screen | King Creole (1958); Blue Hawaii (1961); Viva Las Vegas (1964) | Curatorial proposal. No openly licensed complete edition established; do not add retail streams or assume age makes them reusable. |

Evidence consulted:

- [US Copyright Office: Copyright Horror Stories](https://blogs.loc.gov/copyright/2020/10/copyright-horror-stories/)
  explains how Night of the Living Dead entered the US public domain. This does
  not license a modern restoration or settle worldwide rights.
- [Maryland State Library Resource Center](https://www.slrc.info/research/guides/movie-public-performance-rights/public-domain-movies)
  identifies Night of the Living Dead and Carnival of Souls as notice-failure
  examples and explains the US context.
- [Sony: The Blob](https://www.sonypictures.com/movies/theblob) states copyright
  1958, renewed 1986, Jack H. Harris, all rights reserved. No reuse grant found.
- [Universal: Classic Monsters](https://www.universalpicturesathome.com/movies/universal-classic-monsters-complete-30-film-collection)
  includes Creature from the Black Lagoon and offers retail access, not a
  CATODO integration license.
- [Paramount: Blue Hawaii](https://www.paramountpictures.com/movies/blue-hawaii)
  identifies the 1961 Elvis musical and current restored retail edition. No
  full-film redistribution or integration grant is supplied.
