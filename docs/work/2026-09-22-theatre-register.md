## Admission method

1. Use a filmmaker, rights-holder, institutional publisher or its archived
   distributor page to identify the film-specific grant. A CC footer may apply
   only to website text, raw footage or posters.
2. Match the actual edition: credits, opening/closing grant, release filename,
   duration, language/subtitles, soundtrack and restoration. Uploader tags and
   reachability alone do not establish rights.
3. Probe the actual codec and dimensions, then click the real local Theatre UI
   and require advancing time and decoded frames. Nominal HD filenames can be SD.
4. Preserve complete source media, visible requested attribution and license
   version. Source QR images contain the same authoritative URL.

No film files, streams, subtitles or poster packs are bundled. No DRM, login or
territorial restrictions are bypassed. Several grants are noncommercial; a future
commercial deployment cannot assume this selection is cleared for that use.
Native playback tests do not establish complete-film integrity or physical sound.

## Quality and access limits

Some substantive independent films survive here only as SD archive copies. The
UI labels these honestly; HD availability is not inferred from a filename. Archive
range requests sometimes time out and should be retried by the viewer or opened
on the source page. This selection has no silent-classic edition cleared yet,
so it does not fulfil that editorial part of the request. Externally supplied
subtitle files are not loaded as browser tracks without a verified edition,
attribution and CORS path. Only TPB AFK and The Forbidden Education currently
promise English subtitles within the picture.
