# Archive discovery and CATODO TV

## Discovery provenance

The public [Spelunkr lineup](https://spelunkr.tv/broadcast/lineup.json) was a lead
for Internet Archive item identifiers. The indexer looks those items up in
Internet Archive’s own metadata and applies CATODO categories to the public
video records. The app uses the Archive title and item page. The supplement
contains no station names, membership, programming order, schedule, artwork or
editorial text from Spelunkr. Exact identifiers merge into the existing archive
index; several files can still share one source item. The original external
schedule integration proposal is deferred.

The November 23 run resolved 3,719 public video items from 3,737 identifiers.
Archive marked the remaining 18 as access-restricted. The merge found 434
overlaps and produced an index of 33,352 entries. CATODO TV matched 31 titles
to enabled Featured editions.
Of the 3,719 recordings, 2,780 have at least one category after the merge and
939 remain searchable under Not categorized. Categories can overlap.

## CATODO TV

Theatre includes a standalone shuffle mode for items already playable under an
exact Featured edition. The initial Play gesture starts muted from the source.
Next and Previous follow a shuffled rotation, and ending a recording advances
automatically. Info toggles the programme title, year and editorial context.
The source and edition credits are available below the screen. Leaving CATODO
TV pauses playback and allows manual resume. The viewing sequence is local to
the visit.

## Theatre opening and editorial

Films loads all 334 playable titles on entry and selects a random preview once.
The in-player die uses the same pool and excludes the selected title. The
toolbar randomizer observes the shelf filters. All playable titles have a
written synopsis. The editorial opening has 25 original film notes and 12
original notes for discoveries in the Archive index. Archive titles beyond
the editorial opening retain their existing source metadata and rating order.
The [editorial sequence](../../src/data/theatre-editorial.js) interleaves
animation, street footage, documentaries and historical oddities.
